# Decision-Ready Discovery Guide

This guide helps a prospective client prepare for a useful CompanyConnect strategy or technical discovery conversation. It is designed for business owners, operations leaders, and technical stakeholders who want to decide whether a systems improvement is worth pursuing—and what the responsible first step should be.

## Start with the decision, not the software

The most valuable first conversation is not “Which tool should we buy?” It is “Which operational problem is worth solving now, who owns it, what would better look like, and what constraints must the solution respect?”

| Bring clarity on… | Why it matters | Example of a useful answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bottleneck | Keeps discovery focused on a commercial or operational outcome | “New enquiries are not consistently contacted within one business day.” |
| The people affected | Identifies owners, users, approvers, and customers impacted by the process | “Sales owns the first response; delivery needs a clean handover.” |
| The current handoffs | Reveals where work is delayed, duplicated, or lost | “The form arrives by email, then someone re-enters it into a spreadsheet and a CRM.” |
| The cost of the problem | Helps decide whether the work should be audited, scoped, or deferred | “The team spends several hours weekly reconciling status before management meetings.” |
| The systems involved | Grounds the solution in the current operating environment | “We use Google Workspace, WhatsApp, a CRM, invoicing software, and spreadsheets.” |
| The success measure | Creates a shared definition of progress | “We need every qualified lead assigned, followed up, and visible in a weekly report.” |

## A 30-minute discovery agenda

1. Establish the business outcome and the process owner.
2. Trace the current workflow, including the systems, handoffs, and exception paths involved.
3. Identify the measurable cost, risk, or missed opportunity.
4. Define the future-state principle: ownership, automation, integration, visibility, AI assistance, or a combination.
5. Decide whether a focused build, a broader system, technical discovery, or an audit is the responsible next step.

## Prepare safely

You do not need to bring credentials, customer exports, private documents, or live-system access to an initial conversation. Start with an operational description and representative, non-sensitive examples. If a detailed assessment is appropriate, CompanyConnect will agree a secure way to review the relevant systems and information.
