# Client-reported CRM and workflow implementation evidence

A practical CRM implementation should connect lead intake, follow-up and delivery rather than stop when a deal is marked won. This note distinguishes **what a client publicly reported** from the additional measurement needed to establish an outcome.

## What a published client review supports

The CompanyConnect.Tech [work and reviews page](https://companyconnect.tech/work/#client-reviews) reproduces a review titled **“Monday.com Work Management Board Expert Needed”**, dated **29 September 2025** and attributed to [CompanyConnect's Upwork profile](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/companyconnect).

According to that client account, CompanyConnect's team:
- built out a monday.com CRM and work-management setup;
- connected multiple lead-generation sources using Make.com; and
- linked won opportunities to the project's execution workflow on the same platform.

The client also reported a positive experience with communication and responsiveness. **These are client-reported descriptions, not independently audited operational metrics.** The review does not state a before/after response time, conversion rate, hours saved, revenue improvement or uptime percentage. Client identities and private systems are not reproduced here.

Other reviews currently reproduced on the [work page](https://companyconnect.tech/work/) include **“Seeking Monday.com Expert to Help Finalize PMO Buildout”** (10 March 2025), which describes help with a monday.com PMO and learning the platform, and **“Integration of ChatGPT and HubSpot into Monday.com Platform”** (6 February 2025), which reports positive delivery and communication. Those reviews likewise do not establish audited business outcomes.

## What buyers should verify in a similar project

| Stage | Useful evidence or acceptance check |
| --- | --- |
| Lead intake | Source attribution, required fields, duplicate handling and a named owner |
| CRM handoff | One accepted record, the correct opportunity stage and a recoverable exception path |
| Won-to-delivery transition | Approved scope, accountable project owner and an auditable creation/handoff event |
| Reporting | A clearly defined denominator, observation period and separation of test records from live results |
| Adoption | Staff acceptance, documentation, training and escalation on failures |

To measure actual improvement, agree a comparable baseline and post-launch period before claiming faster follow-up, reduced re-entry or stronger conversion. Obtain written permission for any customer-specific metric or screenshot.
