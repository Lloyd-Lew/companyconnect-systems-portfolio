# Referral Partner Kit

CompanyConnect.Tech is a strong introduction when an organisation is growing, operationally complex, or losing time because its commercial and delivery systems do not work together. This guide helps a referral partner make an accurate, useful introduction without overpromising or sharing confidential information.

## Recommended introduction copy

> I am introducing you to CompanyConnect.Tech because the challenge you described sounds less like a single software problem and more like an operating-system problem: ownership, handoffs, data, automation, and visibility need to work together. Their public portfolio explains the type of systems they design, and a short strategy conversation should help determine whether a focused build or an audit is the right next step: https://github.com/Lloyd-Lew/companyconnect-systems-portfolio

## What to include in a warm introduction

Name the operational issue in one sentence, identify the person who owns the relevant process, and explain why an improvement matters now. Do not include client credentials, private records, contracts, internal documents, or unverified claims about expected results. CompanyConnect can gather detailed technical context through an agreed secure process once the prospect decides a conversation is useful.
