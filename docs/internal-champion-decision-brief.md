# Internal Champion Decision Brief

> **Purpose:** Share this brief internally when you believe CompanyConnect.Tech may be a fit, but need a sponsor, operational owner, finance lead, or technical reviewer to decide whether a discovery conversation is worthwhile.

## What CompanyConnect.Tech helps solve

CompanyConnect.Tech designs CRM, workflow automation, integration, reporting, and AI-enabled operating systems for service businesses that have outgrown disconnected tools, manual handoffs, inconsistent follow-up, or unclear operational visibility.

The work is a fit when the business needs a more reliable way for information, ownership, and status to move across sales, delivery, finance, customer service, and reporting. It is not a generic software-resale conversation; the first question is which operating bottleneck deserves attention and what improvement the business should be able to observe.

## The internal question to answer

| Question | What a useful internal answer looks like |
| --- | --- |
| What is happening today? | “Enquiries are not consistently followed up,” “delivery has incomplete handovers,” or “management reporting requires manual reconciliation.” |
| Why does it matter now? | A measurable cost, risk, delay, rework burden, visibility gap, or growth constraint exists. |
| Who should be involved? | A business sponsor, the process owner, daily users, and technical or data stakeholders where relevant. |
| What should a first conversation decide? | Whether the next route is a focused improvement, a connected operations system, AI discovery, an audit, or technical evaluation. |

## What the initial conversation does not require

An initial conversation does not require a commitment, a system replacement decision, credentials, customer exports, production access, or confidential documents. It should start with the operational context, the relevant stakeholders, the systems involved, and the definition of a better outcome. If detailed technical or data review is needed, it should be agreed and handled through an appropriate secure route.
