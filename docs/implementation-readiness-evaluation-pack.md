# Implementation Readiness and Evaluation Pack

This pack helps a prospective client decide whether a CompanyConnect engagement is worth scoping now, what kind of engagement fits, and what information will make a discovery, audit, or proposal more useful. It is designed for a buying group that includes business, operations, finance, and technical stakeholders.

## The decision to make

Before evaluating tools or providers, agree on the operational decision in front of the business. A useful decision has a named process, a visible cost or risk, an accountable owner, and a practical measure of improvement.

| Evaluation area | Questions to align internally | What a clear answer enables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business outcome | What must improve: response time, handoff reliability, administration, visibility, quality control, or another operating outcome? | A scope anchored in business value rather than a software catalogue. |
| Process boundary | Where does the work begin and end? Which exceptions cannot be ignored? | A realistic workflow map and clearer delivery estimate. |
| Ownership | Who owns the process today, who will make decisions, and who will use the system daily? | Stakeholder alignment, adoption planning, and fewer late-stage surprises. |
| Systems and data | Which CRM, communication, finance, project, spreadsheet, document, or reporting tools are involved? | A grounded integration and data-flow assessment. |
| Risk and constraints | Are there privacy, access, regulatory, timing, budget, or change-management constraints? | Responsible design choices and a safer delivery plan. |
| Success measure | What observable change would make the work worthwhile? | A testable implementation objective and post-launch review. |

## Evidence to request from any implementation partner

Evaluate an implementation partner on more than feature claims. Ask how they will understand the current process, design ownership and exceptions, test the system, protect relevant information, train the team, document the handover, and support the post-launch period.

## Choose the appropriate engagement route

| If your internal position is… | Recommended route |
| --- | --- |
| “We have a defined bottleneck and accountable owner.” | Scope a focused workflow or [Foundation CRM](https://companyconnect.tech/pricing/) engagement. |
| “Several teams, handoffs, systems, and measures are affected.” | Explore a [Revenue & Operations System](https://companyconnect.tech/pricing/). |
| “We need an AI use case but must clarify data, review, and operating safeguards.” | Begin with [AI-Enabled Operations](https://companyconnect.tech/pricing/) discovery or an [AI Business Audit](https://companyconnect.tech/ai-audit/). |
| “We know operations are costly but cannot agree which opportunity comes first.” | Start with an [AI Business Audit](https://companyconnect.tech/ai-audit/). |
| “A technical reviewer needs evidence before authorising discovery.” | Review the [Engineering Hub](https://lloyd-lew.github.io/companyconnect-engineering-hub/) and schedule a technical discovery conversation. |

## Prepare for a scoped conversation

Bring an operational description, a list of relevant tools, a representative non-sensitive example, the current process owner, and the success measure you would use. Do not send credentials, customer exports, contracts, confidential documents, or live-system access in an initial enquiry. If detailed evidence is required, agree an appropriate secure route before sharing it.
