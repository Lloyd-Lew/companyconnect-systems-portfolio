# Sales Team Share Guide

This public portfolio is a proof and education asset. Use it to give a prospective client helpful context before a strategy call, not as a substitute for discovery. The recommended path is simple: share the page that answers the buyer’s immediate question, then invite a conversation about the operational bottleneck behind it.

## Match the prospect to the right resource

| When a prospect says… | Share this | The next conversational step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “What does CompanyConnect.Tech actually do?” | [Portfolio overview](../README.md) | Ask where sales, delivery, or reporting work is currently losing time or context. |
| “We have one process that is clearly painful.” | [Operational Bottleneck Brief](../templates/operational-bottleneck-brief.md) | Ask them to complete the brief, then [book a strategy call](https://calendar.app.google/4SoivAXFkCpeQLVw5). |
| “We know something is wrong, but we are not sure where to begin.” | [Solution menu](solution-menu.md) and [AI Business Audit](https://companyconnect.tech/ai-audit/) | Position the audit as the route to a prioritised, evidence-based roadmap. |

| “Can you show the kind of systems you connect?” | [Representative delivery patterns](../README.md#representative-delivery-patterns) | Explain the closest relevant pattern without sharing client-specific configurations. |

## Approved share copy

### Introductory message

> CompanyConnect.Tech designs CRM, automation, integration, dashboard, and AI operations systems for service businesses. This short portfolio shows the operational problems we solve, the delivery patterns we use, and the best starting point depending on your situation: [portfolio link](https://github.com/Lloyd-Lew/companyconnect-systems-portfolio).

### Follow-up after a discovery conversation

> Based on the handoff and visibility challenges you described, this [Operational Bottleneck Brief](https://github.com/Lloyd-Lew/companyconnect-systems-portfolio/blob/main/templates/operational-bottleneck-brief.md) is the most useful next step. It will help us focus the next discussion on the cost, ownership, tools, and measure that matter—not a generic software list.

### Referral-partner introduction

> I am introducing you to CompanyConnect.Tech because they focus on the systems layer behind growth: CRM ownership, workflow automation, connected tools, reliable reporting, and practical AI with safeguards. Their [public portfolio](https://github.com/Lloyd-Lew/companyconnect-systems-portfolio) gives a concise overview before you decide whether a strategy conversation makes sense.

### Technical buyer or CTO follow-up

> For a look at how we engineer the systems behind these workflows, this [client-safe Lead-to-Quote Automation Demonstrator](https://github.com/Lloyd-Lew/lead-to-quote-automation-demo) includes runnable TypeScript code, architecture, API tests, CI, security controls, and a release. It uses only synthetic data and does not represent a customer implementation.

## Guardrails for sales conversations

Never send a prospect a private repository, raw client export, live workflow link, screenshot of a customer system, credential, or unapproved result claim. This portfolio intentionally uses client-safe delivery patterns; use those patterns to discuss fit, then gather sensitive context through the agreed engagement process.

If the buyer asks for detail that cannot be responsibly shared in public, acknowledge the request, explain that client systems are confidential, and move the conversation to a scoped strategy call or audit.
