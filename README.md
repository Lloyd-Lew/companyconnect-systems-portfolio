## What we build

We configure tools around the way your business actually operates rather than forcing your team into a generic template. Our core work spans **monday.com, Make, Zapier, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, QuickBooks, Xero, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Airtable, Notion, n8n, and custom APIs**.

### CRM and Revenue Operations

We map the full journey from inquiry to retained client, then build the pipeline stages, ownership rules, automations, reports, and adoption plan that keep work moving. This is a strong fit for businesses that need fewer dropped leads and more accountable follow-up.

### Workflow Automation and Integrations

We connect the systems where work is getting lost: forms, email, CRM, project delivery, finance, messaging, and internal operations. The goal is not “more automation”; it is an observable, maintainable workflow with appropriate approvals, exception handling, and documented ownership.

### AI-Enabled Operations

We apply AI to tasks that require interpretation rather than simple rules, including document extraction, lead qualification, support triage, internal knowledge, reporting, and first-draft communication. We use a practical discovery and proof-of-concept process before scaling an AI use case.

## How we work

1. **Discover and map.** We identify the commercial bottleneck, existing systems, data quality, and current handoffs.
2. **Design around outcomes.** We define the future workflow, tool roles, risks, owner responsibilities, and success measures.
3. **Build with safeguards.** We configure the CRM, integrations, automations, and approvals with testing and exception handling.
4. **Launch and enable.** We train the people who use the system, provide documentation, and support a controlled rollout.
5. **Measure and improve.** We review adoption, performance, errors, and new opportunities so the system remains useful as the business changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you work with the software we already use?

Usually. We assess the existing CRM, communication, finance, project, and data tools before recommending whether to configure, integrate, replace, or simplify them. The answer is based on operational fit rather than vendor preference.

### Will we own the system after launch?

Yes. Your business retains ownership of its accounts, process documentation, and configured systems. We train your team so that it can operate the solution confidently.

### What makes an AI workflow safe to use in operations?

An AI workflow needs an appropriate use case, secure access to only the data it needs, clear escalation rules, quality checks, error logging, and named human ownership. We build those controls into the solution design rather than treating AI as an unsupervised add-on.
