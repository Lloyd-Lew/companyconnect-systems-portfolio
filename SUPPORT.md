# Contact and Support

This repository is a public portfolio, not a software product or client-support queue. For a CRM, automation, integration, dashboard, or AI operations enquiry, use the contact route that best matches your stage.

| You need | Best route |
| --- | --- |
| A quick discussion about one operational bottleneck | [Book a free strategy call](https://calendar.app.google/4SoivAXFkCpeQLVw5) |
| A roadmap before committing to implementation | [Explore the AI Business Audit](https://companyconnect.tech/ai-audit/) |
| Package, scope, or investment information | [View implementation packages](https://companyconnect.tech/pricing/) |
| A written enquiry | [letsconnect@companyconnect.tech](mailto:letsconnect@companyconnect.tech) |
| A potential security concern | Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) |

To make a strategy call more useful, complete the [Operational Bottleneck Brief](templates/operational-bottleneck-brief.md) before the conversation. It is optional, but it helps identify the system, handoff, or repeated task worth improving first.
